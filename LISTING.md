# Agensi listing: Money Basics & Scam Shield

**Title (≤ 60):** Money Basics & Scam Shield

**Summary (≤ 300):**
Free money-basics coach and finance-scam shield. Explains loans, cards, interest and inflation in plain words, shows the true cost of a loan, and checks investment, crypto, "forex signals" and loan-app offers for scam signs with a licence check. Educational only, not financial advice.

**Description (≤ 1024):**
Learn money basics and check money offers before you pay, in your own language.

What it does:
- Explains interest, APR, credit cards, loans, microloans, BNPL and inflation in simple words with a tiny example.
- Calculates the true cost of a loan or card: monthly payment, total paid, interest, with the formula shown.
- Checks a pasted offer (Telegram "forex signals", copy-trading, "3% a day" platforms, crypto doubling, pyramids, loan apps, money-mule "jobs") and gives a verdict, quoted red flags, the impossible-return maths and a licence check on the official regulator register.
- If you already paid: ordered recovery steps and a recovery-scam warning.

Where it stops: educational only, not financial advice. Never picks stocks, coins or brokers. Never asks for logins, card or ID numbers. Official figures only from pages it opens, with a date, or marked "verify". Licence checks need web search.

**Categories:** Finance & Accounting (first) · Education & Learning · Security

**Tags:** personal-finance, financial-literacy, scam-detection, fraud-prevention, debt, credit-cards, loans, students, multilingual, free

**Use cases:**
1. Check a Telegram "forex signals" or copy-trading offer before paying
2. Check a "3% a day" platform or crypto doubling offer
3. Calculate how much a loan or microloan really costs
4. See how long paying only the credit-card minimum takes
5. Learn what APR, interest and inflation mean, in your language
6. Check whether an investment company is licensed in your country
7. Get steps after paying an investment or loan-app scammer
8. Spot a money-mule "job"

**Compatibility note:** Works on any agent. Licence checks and official figures need web search; without it, the skill says "not checked" and the best verdict is Suspicious.

**Plan compatibility:** works on Free, Pro, Max and Team plans. Without web search: licence "not checked", best verdict Suspicious, no official figures from memory; paste the register page to continue. Short on messages? Say so and it answers one part at a time.

**Needs access to:** web search / web fetch (optional, for regulator registers).

**Limitations / FAQ:**
- Is this financial advice? No. It explains and calculates; you decide.
- Can it tell me what to invest in? No, never.
- Does it say a company is a scam? It says whether an offer matches scam patterns; only regulators and courts decide.
