# Money basics in plain words (B1)

One idea per sentence. Add a tiny number example. Translate to the user's language; keep the English term in brackets the first time if it helps (e.g. "illik faiz dərəcəsi (APR)").

**Interest** — the price of using someone else's money. You pay it on a loan; a bank pays it on a deposit.
**Simple vs compound interest** — simple: only on the starting amount. Compound: also on interest already added. Compound helps savings and hurts unpaid debt.
**APR / yearly rate** — interest for one year. Monthly ≈ APR / 12. Always ask "per year or per month?".
**Credit card** — a short loan each time you pay with it. Pay the full statement by the due date → often no interest on purchases (check your card's terms). Pay only the minimum → interest on the rest, for a long time. Cash withdrawals: usually fee + interest from day one.
**Debit card** — spends your own money. No loan.
**Loan / credit** — you get money now, pay back more later. Compare the **total paid**, not just the monthly payment.
**Microloan / payday loan** — small, fast, very expensive (often per day). Last option, not first.
**Buy now, pay later / instalments ("0%")** — check fees, late fees and whether the price is higher than paying cash.
**Overdraft** — spending more than you have; usually a high rate.
**Credit history** — record of how you repay. Late payments make future loans harder or more expensive.
**Guarantor** — someone who promises to pay if you don't. Being a guarantor for a friend = you may have to pay their debt.
**Inflation** — prices go up, so the same money buys less. If savings earn less than inflation, they lose value.
**Emergency fund** — money for surprises (phone breaks, a month without income). Start small (one month of essentials), then build up. Keep it where you can reach it, not invested in risky things.
**Saving vs investing vs gambling** — saving: safe, low return. Investing: can grow, can fall; long time; licensed firms only. Gambling/betting: the house wins on average.
**Diversification** — don't put all money in one thing.
**Deposit insurance** — some countries protect bank deposits up to a limit. Give the limit only from the official deposit-insurance page opened now (rule 4); otherwise "verify".
**Licence** — permission from the central bank or securities regulator to take people's money. Check it on the official register, not from the company's own website.
**Debt payoff options** — avalanche: pay extra on the highest rate first (saves most money). Snowball: pay off the smallest balance first (quick wins). Both: pay minimums on all, never skip.
**Never borrow to invest. Never invest rent, tuition or loan money.**

## Golden questions before any offer
1. How exactly does it make money?
2. Who holds my money, and is that firm licensed here?
3. What can I lose? What if I want my money back tomorrow?
4. Why are they contacting me?
