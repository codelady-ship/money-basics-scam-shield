## How to report (order)
1. Platform: Telegram/WhatsApp/Instagram/TikTok "Report"; app store for loan apps.
2. Regulator / central bank of the user's country (official site).
3. Police cybercrime unit (verify the official page for the country).
4. The user's bank or payment service, if money moved.
