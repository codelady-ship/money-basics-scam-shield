# Finance scam patterns

Use these to classify an offer and to name the signals. Quote the user's message for every flag. Never call a named person or company a scammer; say "matches the pattern".

## Quick tests (any one = strong flag)
1. **Fixed or guaranteed high return.** Real investments go up and down. "Guaranteed 20% a month" is a promise no licensed firm can make.
2. **You earn mainly by bringing new people.** Pyramid/matrix.
3. **Pay to withdraw.** "Pay the tax / unlock / verification fee and your profit is released." The profit doesn't exist.
4. **Money goes to a person**, a personal card, a crypto wallet, or a "manager" — not to a licensed company's account in your name.
5. **Upfront fee for a loan.** Real lenders take fees from the loan or in the contract, not as a transfer before the loan.
6. **Paid to receive money and send it on.** Money mule.
7. **Recovery for a fee.** "We recover lost crypto / scam money, pay first."

## Patterns

### Ponzi scheme
Old investors are paid with new investors' money. Signs: steady high "daily/weekly profit"; vague source of profit ("arbitrage", "AI bot", "mining", "trading pool"); early payouts work, then withdrawals "delayed for maintenance"; bonus for reinvesting. Collapse is certain once new money slows.

### Pyramid / "matrix" / disguised MLM
Entry fee or starter pack; income from recruits' fees, levels, "binary" teams. Product is overpriced or an excuse. "Charity" or "mutual help" pyramids also exist (e.g. a 2026 case reported in Uzbekistan). Kazakhstan's regulator lists six signs: inflated returns, compulsory recruiting, no licence, aggressive social-media promotion with gifts, misleading names ("innovative project", "financial centre"), small entry amounts to build trust (gov.kz ARDFM, 2025-07-01).

### Forex / crypto "signals", copy-trading, "account management"
Paid Telegram/WhatsApp/Instagram groups; screenshots of profits; "VIP 95% accuracy"; "give us your trading account login and we manage it"; broker link from the group (often unlicensed or a fake platform). Real facts: most retail leveraged forex/CFD traders lose money; no one can promise accuracy. Asking for your trading login = rule 3 privacy breach and a strong flag.

### Fake trading platform / "AI trading bot"
Nice website, dashboard shows growing balance, small first withdrawal works, then "tax" or "unlock" fee before larger withdrawal. Often foreign "licence" shown. In Azerbaijan, a foreign licence doesn't permit offering investment services locally (Central Bank warning, 2026-08-21).

### Crypto doubling / giveaway / airdrop
"Send 100 USDT, get 200 back", fake celebrity or exchange giveaways, "connect wallet to claim" (wallet drainer). Never send to receive; never share seed phrase.

### Romance / friendship investment ("pig butchering")
A new online friend, often from a "wrong number" message, slowly introduces "my uncle's trading platform". Weeks of trust, then bigger deposits, then withdrawal fees.

### Instant loan apps / advance-fee loans
"Loan without credit history, pay 50 AZN insurance first"; apps that demand access to your contacts and photos, then harass contacts. Banks in some countries don't lend through Telegram or social networks (Uzbekistan central bank statement, news report — verify). Check the lender on the central bank's licensed list.

### Money mule "jobs"
"Easy job: receive transfers to your card and send them on, keep 10%." Or "sell your bank card/account". It's laundering stolen money; the account owner can be held responsible.

### Fake currency exchange / P2P
Better-than-bank exchange rate from a person on Telegram; unlicensed exchange is illegal in Azerbaijan (Central Bank, 2020). P2P crypto trades where the buyer's money is stolen ("triangulation").

### Betting "fixed matches", "guaranteed predictions"
Sold tips; if they knew results, they wouldn't sell them.

### Recovery scams
After a loss, "lawyers" or "hackers" contact you to recover money for a fee. Second scam.

## Medium flags
Unsolicited contact; pressure ("only today", "last 5 places"); secrecy ("don't tell your bank"); profit screenshots; celebrity endorsement or fake news article; "risk-free"; can't explain how money is made; minimum to start is small then grows; only crypto accepted; support only via Telegram; company registered abroad with no local licence; licence number that doesn't match the register.

## Impossible-return maths (show it)
- Daily rate d for 30 days compounding: (1+d)^30. Example 3%/day → 1.03^30 ≈ 2.43, i.e. +143% a month. For a year: 1.03^365 ≈ 48,500×. No business earns this.
- Monthly rate m for a year: (1+m)^12. Example 10%/month → 1.1^12 ≈ 3.14, i.e. +214% a year.
- Compare with a figure the user can check: the bank deposit rate or central-bank rate **from an official page opened now** (rule 4), otherwise "compare with your bank's deposit rate (verify)".
