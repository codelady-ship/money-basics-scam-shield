## Case 3

Pages opened: https://www.cbar.az/page-200/investment-companies, https://www.trend.az/business/4216829.html

Don't pay the 100 USDT.

```
VERDICT: Likely scam
Red flags:
• "You pay 100 USDT to join" + "50 USDT for every person you bring" — pay to join, earn by recruiting: the pyramid / "matrix" pattern.
• "plus a level bonus" — income from levels of recruits, not from a real product.
• "licence from Seychelles" — a foreign licence doesn't allow offering investment services in Azerbaijan (Central Bank warning, 2026-08-21).
• Pay in USDT (a crypto "stablecoin") — crypto-only payment is a medium flag; it's hard to get back.
Maths: you need 2 recruits just to get your 100 USDT back. Each of them needs 2, and so on: level 10 = 1,024 people, level 33 = 2^33 ≈ 8.6 billion — more than everyone on Earth. Recruiting stops long before that, so most people at the bottom lose.
Licence: not found — "Smart Matrix Club" isn't on the Central Bank of Azerbaijan list of licensed investment companies (12 firms) — https://www.cbar.az/page-200/investment-companies (opened 2026-11-11)
Safe next step: Don't invite anyone. Tell your friend about the Central Bank warning.
Report: report the group/chat on the platform · Central Bank of Azerbaijan (cbar.az) · police cybercrime unit (verify the official page)
```

This matches the pattern of a financial pyramid. CBA warning: only firms it licenses may offer investment services in Azerbaijan; a foreign regulator's licence isn't enough — https://www.trend.az/business/4216829.html (opened 2026-11-11, news report of the CBA statement).
